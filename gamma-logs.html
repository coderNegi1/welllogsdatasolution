LEGACY RESTORATION

Conversion of Old Gamma Logs

Specialized handling and depth-matching digitization for historical, weathered, or non-standard legacy gamma ray logs.

Unlocking Value from Historic Gamma Ray Records

Legacy gamma logs often suffer from physical paper degradation, unusual scales, non-linear tracks, or fading prints. Discarding these records means losing critical stratigraphic context.

Our specialists use custom image enhancement and manual verification workflows to convert old, weathered gamma logs into clean, standardized digital curves ready for correlation.
